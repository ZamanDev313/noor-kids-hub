💚 About Noor Kids Hub

A little digital garden built for the children of Pakistan, and Muslim kids everywhere.

🎯

Our Mission

To make learning Islam feel joyful instead of like a chore — with games, colors, and encouragement instead of fear or boredom.

🇵🇰

Built for Pakistan

Designed with Pakistani Muslim families in mind — familiar greetings, Urdu touches, and content that feels like home.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Kids & Adults Together

While games are made for kids, parents and older learners are welcome too — the Learn section is for the whole family.

Why we made this

Too many kids think learning about Islam means sitting still and memorizing quietly. We wanted to flip that around: bright colors, cheerful sounds, badges to collect, and a friendly moon mascot cheering every child on. Every quiz, every matching game, and every lesson here is designed so a child finishes feeling proud — not scared or bored.

What's coming next, inshaAllah

This is just the beginning. We're building this site first in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — and inshaAllah, we'll soon rebuild it with React for an even smoother, richer experience. More games, more languages (including Urdu), and more lessons are on the way.

🌙

JazakumAllahu Khairan for being part of our journey — every star you earn here is a step towards good habits, inshaAllah.

— The Noor Kids Hub Team
