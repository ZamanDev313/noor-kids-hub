📜 Rules & Safety

A happy place stays happy when everyone follows a few simple rules. Please read these together as a family.

🧒 Rules for Kids

  • 1Always start with "Bismillah" and be kind while you play and learn.
  • 2Ask a parent or guardian before you spend time on any device, including this website.
  • 3Take breaks — play for a little while, then rest your eyes and stretch.
  • 4Never share your full name, address, phone number, or photos anywhere online.
  • 5If a game feels frustrating, that's okay — take a breath, and remember Allah loves those who are patient (Sabr).
  • 6Celebrate your badges, but remember it's the good habits that truly matter, not just the score.
  • 7Be honest — don't peek at answers, just try your best and learn from mistakes.

🕌 Islamic Etiquette (Adab) We Practice Here

  • 1We speak about Allah, the Prophets, and the Qur'an with respect and love.
  • 2We don't show pictures or drawings of the Prophets or their companions, out of respect for Islamic tradition.
  • 3We treat every lesson as something to practice, not just something to memorize for points.
  • 4We encourage kids to ask their parents, teachers, or a local Imam if they have deeper questions.
  • 5We celebrate all Muslim traditions with respect, focusing on what unites us as one Ummah.

👨‍👩‍👧 Rules & Notes for Parents

  • 1All progress (XP, badges, scores) is stored locally in your child's browser using LocalStorage — nothing is uploaded to a server or shared with third parties.
  • 2There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no external chat features anywhere on this site.
  • 3Content is written to be simple and mainstream, suitable for children learning the basics of Islam. It is not a substitute for guidance from a qualified teacher or Imam.
  • 4Since progress is saved per-browser and per-device, clearing browser data or switching devices will reset a child's XP and badges.
  • 5We recommend supervising screen time and using this site together as a family activity when possible.
🤲

Following these simple rules keeps Noor Kids Hub a joyful, safe place for everyone — jazakumullahu khairan for reading!

— The Noor Kids Hub Team
